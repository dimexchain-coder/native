What is DimeX Chain (DMXC)?

DimeX Chain is an enterprise-grade, EVM-compatible Layer-1 native blockchain. Built on QBFT (Istanbul-based Proof of Authority and Fault Tolerance) consensus, DimeX guarantees instant transaction execution without network congestion or high gas fees.

Unlike traditional tokens layered on third-party chains, DMXC is the true native fuel powering smart contracts, validator rewards, cross-chain DEX liquidity, and native level-based staking protocols.
